Cost Anomalies

Days on which a service or environment cost noticeably more than its recent baseline.

How anomalies are detected

Rule-based, not machine learning. For each service and each environment, every day's cost is compared with the median daily cost of the previous 14 calendar days that have data (at least 7 are required). A day is flagged when the increase is at least 50% and at least $25.00, so small fluctuations are ignored. Severity follows the absolute increase: medium from $100, high from $500. Detection runs automatically after every import.
