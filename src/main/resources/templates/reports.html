File formats

Summary CSV: one table with the columns section, name, value, comparison_value, change, change_percent, percent, status, date, notes. A service or environment filter applies to the total and the breakdowns and is recorded in a filter metadata row. The section column identifies report metadata, the period total, each service, each environment, budgets (value = spend, comparison_value = limit, percent = utilization), anomaly findings (value = observed, comparison_value = baseline) and recommendations (value = estimated monthly savings, empty when not estimated). Amounts use the reporting currency with two decimals; percentages are empty where undefined.

Daily costs CSV: date, service, environment, amount, currency, one row per day, service and environment with stored cost.

Anomalies CSV: one row per finding with its date, dimension, observed and baseline amounts, increase, severity, review status and note. Recommendations CSV: one row per recommendation with its category, severity, status, scope, estimated monthly savings and basis, evidence and suggested action.
